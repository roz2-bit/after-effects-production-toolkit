# Audio handoff

## Editorial handoff

Keep dialogue, music, effects, and guide tracks identifiable. Use markers for important sync points and write the source timecode or offset in the notes.

- [ ] Confirm sample rate, channel layout, loudness target, and head/tail handles.
- [ ] Separate guide audio from final audio; label muted tracks.
- [ ] Check sync after frame-rate conversion and time remapping.
- [ ] Report whether audio is embedded, sidecar, or intentionally absent.
- [ ] Do not include unlicensed music or client recordings in this repository.

For a silent graphics deliverable, state `AUDIO: NONE` rather than leaving intent ambiguous. For a final mix, request the approved WAV/stems and preserve the supplied filename and license record.
