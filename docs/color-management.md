# Color management

Color decisions must be explicit and repeatable.

## Working agreement

- Record the working space, display assumptions, input interpretation, output transform, and bit depth.
- Interpret footage deliberately; do not rely on an automatic guess for camera or graphics files.
- Keep review exports labeled when they use a different transform from final delivery.
- Check skin tones, saturated brand colors, gradients, blacks, and highlights on a calibrated or agreed display.
- Avoid mixing unmanaged screenshots into color decisions without noting their source.

## Delivery checklist

- [ ] All inputs have an interpretation or are explicitly marked unmanaged.
- [ ] The comp and output module use the approved color policy.
- [ ] HDR/SDR, legal/full range, and alpha expectations are written down.
- [ ] A representative test render was compared with the reference.
- [ ] The final output metadata and transform are included in the manifest.

When the pipeline is uncertain, deliver a short labeled test before a long final render.
