# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or affiliation.

## Expected behavior

Use clear, constructive language. Assume good intent, explain disagreement with evidence, respect boundaries, and give credit. Keep client information and unreleased work confidential.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and sharing confidential or infringing material are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the contact route listed in the repository settings. Include links or context when safe. Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect confidentiality where possible, and may remove content or restrict participation.
